Privacy Policy - Closer

Last Updated: May 5, 2026

IMPORTANT: This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use Closer. By using our service, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Automatically Collected Information

When you access Closer, we automatically collect:

  • IP Address: Your internet connection identifier
  • Date and Time: Exact moment of access
  • Device Information: Basic information about your device (brand, model)
  • Browser Information: Browser type, version, and operating system
  • Access Patterns: How you interact with our service

1.2 Information You Provide

When you create an account or use our service, you may provide:

  • Personal Information: Name, email, date of birth
  • Profile Information: Gender, interests, preferences
  • Location Information: Country, state, city
  • Content: Photos, messages, and other user-generated content
  • Communication Data: Messages and interactions with other users

2. Purpose of Data Collection

2.1 Security and Protection

  • Mutual Security: Prevent fraud and unauthorized access
  • Account Protection: Ensure your account is not used by third parties
  • Legal Compliance: Maintain records as required by law
  • Platform Safety: Protect all users from harmful activities

2.2 Service Improvement

  • User Experience: Improve app functionality and performance
  • Personalization: Provide relevant content and matches
  • Analytics: Understand how users interact with our service
  • Feature Development: Create new features based on user needs

3. Data Storage and Security

3.1 Storage Duration

Access logs and security data are maintained for minimum 6 months as required by applicable laws. User account data is retained until account deletion.

3.2 Security Measures

  • Encryption: End-to-end encryption for sensitive communications
  • Secure Storage: Data stored in secure, access-controlled environments
  • Regular Audits: Security audits and penetration testing
  • Access Controls: Limited access to user data
  • Monitoring: 24/7 security monitoring

4. Legal Compliance

4.1 Applicable Laws

We comply with:

  • GDPR: General Data Protection Regulation (European users)
  • LGPD: Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados (Brazilian users)
  • PDPA: Personal Data Protection Act (Southeast Asian users)
  • Local Laws: Data protection laws in target regions

4.2 Legal Requirements

We are legally required to:

  • Maintain access logs for minimum 6 months
  • Provide data to authorities upon valid legal request
  • Report suspicious activities to relevant authorities
  • Cooperate with law enforcement investigations

5. Your Rights

5.1 Data Access and Control

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request information about your stored data
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and data
  • Portability: Request your data in a portable format
  • Restriction: Limit processing of your data

5.2 How to Exercise Rights

To exercise your data rights, contact us at:

  • Email: privacy@closer.com
  • Subject: "Data Rights Request"

6.2 Limited Sharing

We may share data only when:
